Bearing faults are a common cause of induction motor failure.

A bearing defect can produce characteristic vibration patterns.
Common symptoms include increased vibration amplitude, abnormal
noise, temperature increase, and vibration components associated
with the bearing defect frequencies.

Regular vibration monitoring can help detect bearing degradation
before complete bearing failure occurs.

If a bearing fault is suspected, the motor should be inspected
and the bearing condition should be evaluated by a qualified
maintenance engineer.
